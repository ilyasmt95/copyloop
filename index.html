Questions

Does Copyloop upload what I copy?

No. Your history is stored in Chrome's local storage on your device and is never sent to us or anyone else. The only network requests are price and plan-status checks with our payment provider, which never include your copies.

What happens to older copies on the free plan?

They're kept on your device, just hidden. The free plan shows what you copied today. If you upgrade, your whole history appears straight away.

Does it work with "Copy" buttons on websites?

Yes. Copy buttons on AI chats, code blocks, share links and similar are saved, as long as you clicked them.

Can it save links I copy from the address bar?

Yes, as an option you turn on in Settings. Chrome will ask to let Copyloop read the clipboard, and only addresses matching the open tab are kept.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

Subscriptions can be cancelled anytime, and you keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. Full details are in the refund and cancellation policy.

I paid on another computer. How do I get Pro here?

Open Copyloop's settings and choose "Restore your purchase", then enter the email you paid with.

What happens if I remove Copyloop?

Your history is deleted, because it's stored only on your computer. Save a backup first (Settings, "Back up and restore"), or just turn Copyloop off instead of removing it.

Can I move my history to another computer?

Yes. Save a backup in Settings, "Back up and restore", then restore that file on the other computer. Your copies keep their original dates.
